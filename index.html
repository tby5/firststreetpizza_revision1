Our Story

Built on Passion

First Street Pizza & Espresso exists because we believed Downtown San Jose deserved better. Better pizza, better espresso, better beer, and better community.

We're a neighborhood joint that refuses to compromise. Every pizza is hand-stretched moments before it hits the oven. Every espresso shot is pulled with intention. Every beer is chosen because we genuinely love it.

Our regulars know they'll get the same warm welcome whether it's their first visit or their hundredth. That consistency, that care, that's what sets us apart. We're not chasing trends—we're building something that lasts.

Whether you're fueling up for work with our signature cappuccino, grabbing a slice at lunch, unwinding with happy hour pints every Monday through Thursday (4-6pm with 50% off all draft beers), or joining us late-night when the game is on—you belong here.
