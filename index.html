Our Story

Built on Passion

At First Street Pizza & Espresso, we believe that a delicious, authentic pizza shouldn't be hard to find.

We're a new neighborhood joint that hopes to prove ourselves worthy of becoming a staple in Downtown San Jose. Every pizza is hand-stretched moments before it hits the oven. Every espresso shot is pulled with intention. Every beer is chosen because we genuinely love it.

Our regulars know they'll get the same warm welcome whether it's their first visit or their hundredth. That consistency and that care are what set us apart. We're not chasing trends, we're building something that lasts.
