Our Story

Built on Passion

At First Street Pizza & Espresso, we believe that a delicious, authentic pizza shouldn't be hard to find.

We're a new neighborhood joint that hopes to prove ourselves worthy of becoming a staple in Downtown San Jose. Every pizza is hand-stretched moments before it hits the oven. Every espresso shot is pulled with intention. Every beer is chosen because we genuinely love it.

Our regulars know they'll get the same warm welcome whether it's their first visit or their hundredth. That consistency and that care are what set us apart. We're not chasing trends, we're building something that lasts.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your hours?

We're open Thursday through Sunday from 3:00 PM to midnight. We're closed Monday through Wednesday.

Where is First Street Pizza & Espresso located?

We're at 33 S. 1st St, San Jose, CA 95113, in Downtown San Jose. You can call us at (408) 352-5371 or get directions in Google Maps.

Do you deliver?

Yes. You can order our pizza, wings and more for delivery through DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub.

Do you sell pizza by the slice?

Yes. We offer Margherita, pepperoni and cheese slices for $6.50 each.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Try our Garden Veggies Pizza, Classic Margherita or Premium Cheese Pizza, or the Insalata Della Casa salad. Ask us if you have any questions about ingredients.

What wing flavors do you offer?

We serve Ethiopian Berbere, Buffalo and BBQ wings in 6, 12 or 24 piece orders.

What can I drink at First Street?

The Grind Bar pours coffee, espresso drinks, matcha, chai and iced drinks. We also have draft and bottled beer plus red and white wine.
